Eat more protein! We need protein! Let's face it, with inflation increasing with exercising more, a higher protein intake is required. So what is the first thing we think of? Protein powders! Cost of protein powders have inncreased in the market by over 10-20$ or even more. Do we really need these processed powder in our bodies? IWhy are there so many companies producing these powders. Isn't it better to spend your money on natural foods instead?

There are serval ways to make great protein shakes without the need of protein powders. Not only your wallet will benefit from it but your body will get the extra nutruients that it requires. Protein powders in the market are mostly all processed and depending on the brand some of them add ingredients that we can't even read! Why drink it? Here is one receipe I will show you and talk about the health benefits.

## Recipe  

- Bananna
- Frozen or fresh blueberries
- Pumpkin Seeds
- Hemp Seeds
- Natural Peanut Butter or Nut Butter
- Milk or Almond & Cashew Milk
- Cinnamon (optiona)
- Greek yogurt (optional)
- Raw egg (optional and if your in the Rocky Balboa mood!)

For quantites of each ingredient depends on the macros and the taste your aiming for. For example I like 2 tablespoons of nut butter. If you want one because of the calorie intake, then add one. You want 2 banannas? Well have fun. Like I said, if your calculating your macros add the quantity you need. You don't like blueberries, add strawberries! Actually bananna and strawberries are another great combination. Or even better add all three!

If your not into milk and it's fine. You switch to almond or oat milk if you wish. I choose almond & cashew milk as it contains 8g of protien for 1 cup. It all depends on your goals. If your trying to loose fat then go with almond milk which contains less calories. If you want to gain mass then go for milk. 

I love greek yogurt which contains lots of protein but if you don't like animal products then you can leave it out. This ingredient will give a creamy taste.

Pumpkin and hemp seeds contains good protein that required only a small amount of it. Maybe about one tablespoon of each. Not only these have high in protein but also contains zinc, potassium, Omega-3 that the body needs.

## Final

For myself, I squeeze around 700-900 calories with about 30-35 grams of protein. Hoping you will try this shake and enjoy it and leaving those protein powders containers on the shelves. Probably you will have to adjust to your liking, maybe removing or adding an ingredient. Make this your breakfast meal as it will fill you up for a few hours. Remember don't have this before diner or else you will spoil your appetite.
