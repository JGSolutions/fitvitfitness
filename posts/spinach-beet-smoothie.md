This is not typical high protein shake as my previous blog article on  [Homemade Shakes Are Better Than Protein Powders](https://fitvitfitness.com/posts/homemade-shakes-better-than-protein-powders/) but a healthy, antioxidant-packed smoothie option, great for after a workout. Beets are usually lacking people's diet and this smoothie is an easy way to increase your intake. If you want more protein, you may always add greek yogurt and hemp seeds.

## Why You’ll Love this Beet Smoothie 

Despite being filled with hidden veggies, this healthy beet smoothie is sweet and delicious. The beets earthy flavor with an apple and banana give it a creamy and sweet delicious taste or add blueberries if you wish to make it sweeter. It’s packed with nutritional benefits that will energize you for the rest of the day!  

I love fresh smoothies as they are a great way to sneak some nutritious vegetables and fresh fruit into your diet especially after a workout! 

## Health Benefits of Beets

Here are reasons why I use beet:

- High in vitamin C, which is always a welcome boost to our immune system
- High in fiber
- Loaded with minerals such as manganese and potassium
- Contains folate
- Lower blood pressure

## Smoothie Ingredients  

- Spinach
- 1 Beet (raw and peeled)
- 1 Banana
- Apple
- Ginger
- Frozen or fresh blueberries (optional)
- Green grape (optional)
- Greek Yougurt (optional)

Throw all ingredients in a blender, add water, blend and enjoy!

## Conclusion

I hope you try it and enjoy it! This is a great option if you want to tone down and lower your calorie intake. As this smoothie contains high in fiber that will keep you filled up and less hunger through out the day. I even recommend having this twice day especially if your busy and on the go eveyday!
